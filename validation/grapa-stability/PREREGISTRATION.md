## 1. The system, from code

Per tick: `z_t = clip((x_t − μ̂)/(3σ̂), −1, 1)` (AR(1)-whitened when φ is supplied);
bet `λ_t = m̄_{t−1}/s̄_{t−1}` (GRAPA, `grapaBet`) where `m̄, s̄` are running first and second
moments of `z` with `1/n` gain; ONS fallback when `|λ| > 1 − 10⁻⁶` (`pickBet`); wealth advances
by `log(1 + λ_t z_t)` in the log domain. This is a stochastic-approximation feedback loop with
decaying gain wrapped around a multiplicative wealth process — the growth-rate lens is
`stats/wealth-growth-rate`.

## 2. Derivations, frozen as registered predictions

**P1 — bet convergence under exact H₀** (iid Gaussian, oracle μ/σ; battery cell N1). `m̄_n` is a
sample mean of centered z, so `m̄_n ≈ N(0, s/n)` with `s = E[z²]`; `s̄_n → s`. Hence
`λ_n = m̄_n/s̄_n` has mean 0 and `Var(λ_n) ≈ 1/(s·n)`. **Registered:** the OLS slope of
`log Var(λ_n)` on `log n` over `n ∈ {50, 100, 200, 300}` lies in **[−1.3, −0.7]**, and
`|mean(λ_300)| < 2·se`.

**P2 — the H₀ wealth-decay law.** λ_t is predictable and `E[z_t] = 0`, so
`E[Δ log M_t] ≈ −½·E[λ_t²]·s ≈ −1/(2t)`: cumulative `E[log M_n] ≈ −½·log n` — polynomial decay,
NOT exponential; the 1/n gain makes the bet die fast enough that the Jensen tax integrates to
half a log. **Registered:** the OLS slope of `E[log M_n]` on `log n` over the same grid lies in
**[−0.75, −0.25]**.

**P3 — validity at oracle parameters is exact.** λ_t predictable and `E[1 + λz] = 1` make `M_n`
a martingale: the increment estimator `E[exp(Δ log M)]` (the audit's validity instrument) reads
1 within noise. **Registered:** its pooled value on N1 lies in **[0.9995, 1.0005]** — the C23
CLEARED reproduced by this harness.

**P4 — the estimation-extraction law, the quantitative headline.** With μ̂ from an m-sample
calibration, the standardized z carries a per-draw bias `b = −ε/(3σ)`, `ε ~ N(0, σ²/m)`. The
loop CONVERGES ON THE BIAS: `λ_∞ ≈ b/s`, and the per-tick increment mean becomes
`E[e_t] ≈ 1 + λ_∞·b = 1 + b²/s`. Averaging over the calibration draw, `E[b²] = 1/(9m)` and
`s = E[z²] ≈ 1/9` (Gaussian, B = 3, clip negligible), so the **excess is ≈ 1/m** — derived, not
fitted. Prior evidence: C23 measured 1.029 / 1.009 / 1.002 at m = 30/100/500
(`stats/detector-audit-sequential-2026-08-05`), i.e. κ̂ ≈ 0.87 against the first-principles
κ = 1; the shortfall plausibly belongs to the pre-convergence transient and σ̂ error.
**Registered:** fitting `excess = κ/m` across m ∈ {30, 100, 500} (battery construction N2, σ̂
and μ̂ both estimated, as deployed), **κ ∈ [0.7, 1.1]** with per-m residuals under 25%.

**P5 — clip stability on the shapes that killed the mixture.** `|λ| ≤ 1 − 10⁻⁶` and `|z| ≤ 1`
bound the log-increment in `[log(10⁻⁶), log 2] ≈ [−13.8, 0.69]` — no finite-sample blowup is
possible, unlike the mixture's unbounded increments (NaN at N5, 8.5×10⁴⁶ at N6, C23).
**Registered:** on N5 (lognormal) and N6 (t₃), zero non-finite log-increments across all
trajectories, max |log-increment| ≤ 13.9, and fire rate at α = 0.05 in **[0, 0.10]**.

**P6 — the ONS fallback is a startup device.** At `n = 1`, `λ = z₁/z₁² = 1/z₁` with `|z₁| ≤ 1`,
so GRAPA leaves the unit ball on essentially every trajectory and the first bet is ONS's.
**Registered:** fallback occurs within the first 2 evaluable ticks on **≥ 95%** of N1
trajectories, and the fallback rate over ticks 101–300 is **≤ 5%**.

## 3. Cells, sizes, instrument

- Cells: N1 (oracle), N2-m30/m100/m500 (estimated μ̂, σ̂ — the h0-battery construction,
  calibration drawn per trajectory from the same stream), N5, N6. All disjoint-free scalar
  cells; nulls generated by `validation/h0-battery/harness/nulls.mjs` generators verbatim.
- N = 2000 trajectories × T = 300 ticks per cell; per-tick α = 0.05 for the descriptive fire
  rate. Seeds: per-stream splitmix64 (`validation/family-d-emean/harness/seed.mjs`), scheme in
  the manifest.
- The instrument drives `dist/detectors/betting-e-process.js` (`updateBettingState`) and reads
  the detector's OWN state per tick (`state.bet`, `state.log_M`, `state.onsFallbackCount`) —
  never a reimplementation of the dynamics.
- Append-only `results/live/run-<UTC>/`; cells carry no stage-readable fields
  (no exceedance/mean_e/detection_rate/rate_e_ge_20/fault_class), so certification verdicts
  cannot move; the corpus census grows by the cell count with arithmetic recorded, per the
  standing pattern.

## 4. Endpoints and verdicts

Each of P1–P6 scores independently: **HELD** iff every registered band in it is met, else
**REFUTED** — no post-run band moves, per the discipline. A REFUTED prediction is a finding
about the dynamics, not a failure of the study.

## 5. Not-executable

If the harness cannot reproduce the detector's own trajectory — checked by comparing the
harness-observed `state.bet` sequence against a second independent drive of the same seeds —
the instrument is wrong and nothing is scored.
